What this site is

This is the public information page for the PieceOfInsult application. It exists so that people who see this app's name on a Google sign-in screen can find out what it does and read its policies before granting access.

What the app does

PieceOfInsult is a single-developer tool that publishes short-form videos to the PieceOfInsult YouTube channel. It is a convenience client for one content channel — it is not a public service, and it does not offer accounts to other people.

Concretely, the app can:

  • Upload videos — title, description, tags and thumbnail, to the developer's own channel.
  • Read basic channel and video metadata — to confirm that an upload succeeded and to report its status.

Why it requests access to your Google data

Uploading a video to a YouTube channel requires authorization from the Google account that owns that channel. The app therefore asks for exactly two YouTube Data API v3 scopes:

  • youtube.upload — upload videos to the channel.
  • youtube.readonly — read channel and video metadata to verify the upload.

The app accesses Google user data only when the developer runs an upload. It is never presented to visitors of this site, and it is never requested from you unless you are the app's developer.

It requests no other Google product — no Gmail, no Google Drive, no Calendar, no contacts, no location, no advertising data.

How data is handled

  • The refresh token and access token are stored only on the developer's own machine, in local credential files.
  • Uploaded video metadata (title, description, tags) is stored on the developer's own systems and on YouTube.
  • Nothing collected by this app is sold, brokered, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The developer can revoke access at any time from Google Account permissions, and access can be revoked here by clicking Remove access on the sign-in screen.

Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Source of the content

The channel's video scripts are built from quotations retrieved from published sources, with the source URL recorded alongside each quotation. Attribution is checked and known misattributions are flagged rather than repeated.
