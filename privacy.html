Privacy Policy

PieceOfInsult · effective 26 October 2026

This policy describes how the PieceOfInsult application ("the app", "we") handles information. It is a single-developer tool that publishes short-form videos to the developer's own YouTube channel.

1. Google user data the app accesses

The app requests only these YouTube Data API v3 scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to upload a video, including its title, description, tags, thumbnail and privacy status, to the channel owned by the authorizing account.
  • youtube.readonly — to read basic channel and video metadata after an upload, so the developer can confirm that it succeeded and report its video ID and status.

The app does not access Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, contacts, location, advertising data, YouTube comments, private videos it does not own, or any other Google product.

2. How Google user data is used

Data is used for one purpose only: to carry out the upload the developer requested, and to verify that it completed. It is not used to profile any person, to personalise advertising, or to build an audience or marketing list.

3. How Google user data is stored

  • OAuth credentials — the refresh token and access token are written to local credential files on the developer's own machine, with user-only file permissions. They are not uploaded to any server or service.
  • Video metadata — titles, descriptions and tags are held in the developer's own local content files, and are also held by YouTube.
  • Tokens are retained until access is revoked or the credential files are deleted.

4. Sharing and sale

Google user data is never sold, rented, brokered or shared with third parties. It is not transferred to any advertising or analytics provider. The only recipient is Google, through the YouTube API, for the upload itself.

Information may be disclosed only where the developer is legally required to do so.

5. Data this site collects

This website is static. It contains no analytics, no tracking pixels, no cookies, no account system and no forms. It collects nothing about its visitors.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Google user data is retained only for as long as the developer needs it to run and verify the channel, and until access is revoked.
  • To delete everything, revoke the app's access at Google Account > Third-party access. Revocation removes the tokens immediately; the developer's local copies can then be deleted.
  • Uploaded videos remain on YouTube until deleted from YouTube.

7. Security

Access is OAuth 2.0 — the app never sees or stores your Google password. Credentials are stored locally with restrictive file permissions. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so the developer keeps the requested scope to the minimum listed above.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes

This policy will be updated here when the app's data handling changes. The effective date at the top will change with it.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact address published on the app's Google Cloud project.
